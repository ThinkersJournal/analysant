Gate B verdict capture and the stop-rule tracker: `analysant judge append` (one verdict per line into a verdict table; a false verdict names a noise class, every verdict has a reason, one judge cannot judge one finding twice, a second judge's verdict is kept beside the first), `analysant judge report` (per kind: judged to the stop point, true, false, unjudgeable counted false, precision, the rule-of-three bound, the stop reason, the 30 clause, whether the kind clears, noise-class counts, and second-judge agreement `k of n`), and `analysant judge sheet` (the blind items for the second judge). The pass line is a required argument with no default and the agreement threshold is unset until CireSnave sets it (D1). Library: `analysant::judging`. No new dependencies. `docs/GATE-B-PROTOCOL.md` section 7 step 9 and 10 describe the commands. `reidentify` is not wired into `extract_corpus` (two id schemes would meet; see the spec's "Judging result").
